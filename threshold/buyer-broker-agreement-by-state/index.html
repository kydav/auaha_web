One nationwide rule, local paperwork

Since August 17, 2024, agents who participate in a Multiple Listing Service must have a written buyer agreement signed before touring a home with a buyer — in person or on a live virtual tour — and it must state a specific, objective compensation amount. That baseline applies across the country.

What still varies is the form. Your state real estate commission, your state REALTOR® association, your local board or MLS, and your brokerage can each require a particular buyer-broker agreement and mandate its use. Threshold builds those forms in for the states it supports; everywhere else, confirm the right document with the bodies that govern you.

  • Signed before touring — the trigger is showing a specific home.
  • A specific compensation amount — objective and agreed, not open-ended.
  • The form is local — check your state association, board/MLS, or brokerage for which one to use.
