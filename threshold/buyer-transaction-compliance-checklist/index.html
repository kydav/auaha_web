Why this checklist

Post-2024, a compliant buyer transaction has more moving parts than it used to — starting with a signed written buyer agreement before the first showing. This checklist walks the buyer side from that first agreement through closing and recordkeeping, so nothing slips between a busy showing schedule. It's a general best-practice guide; your state and brokerage may add steps.

The one step to never skip

A written buyer agreement — signed, dated, and stating a specific compensation amount — needs to be in place before you tour a home with a buyer. That single document is where most compliance risk now lives. Threshold makes it fast in Colorado, Oklahoma, Louisiana, and Wisconsin.
