What changed for Colorado agents

Colorado has long required a defined, written brokerage relationship before an agent represents a buyer. Since August 17, 2024, agents who work through an MLS also must have a signed buyer agreement in place before touring a home — in person or virtually. That makes getting the Exclusive Right-to-Buy contract signed early a routine part of every Colorado buyer relationship.

What your written buyer agreement needs

  • A signature before touring. Agents working through an MLS need the agreement signed before showing a home, in person or virtually.
  • A specific compensation amount. The figure must be objective and agreed with your buyer — not open-ended, and not more than you and the buyer set.
  • Clear terms and dates. The scope of representation and the time period should be spelled out, using Exclusive Right-to-Buy Brokerage Contract.
  • The buyer's signature on file. Keep a signed copy you can produce for your brokerage or a compliance check.

Colorado buyer agreement FAQ

Do Colorado real estate agents need a written buyer agreement?

Yes. Colorado requires a defined, written brokerage relationship for buyer representation, and since August 17, 2024 agents working through an MLS must have a signed buyer agreement before touring a home. Threshold makes it quick to complete and sign that agreement on the spot.

Which Colorado buyer agreement form does Threshold use?

Threshold is built around the Colorado Real Estate Commission's Exclusive Right-to-Buy Brokerage Contract — the standard buyer agency form Colorado brokerages use. Fields are guided so nothing required is left blank.

Can I sign a buyer agreement on my phone in Colorado?

Yes. You and your buyer can complete every required field and capture signatures on-screen, then export a PDF to send to your brokerage or client. No printer or scanner needed.

Does the agreement have to state my compensation?

Yes. The written buyer agreement must state a specific, objective compensation amount agreed with your buyer — it can't be open-ended. Threshold has a dedicated compensation step so the figure is captured clearly every time.
