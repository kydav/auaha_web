Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Threshold for? ▾
Threshold is designed for licensed real estate agents and brokers, particularly those working in Colorado. It helps you complete buyer representation agreements digitally — including the Colorado BC60 form — without paper or delays.
How do I create an account? ▾
Open the app and tap "Sign Up." Enter your email address and create a password. You'll receive a verification email — confirm it and you're ready to go.
Is the BC60 form always up to date? ▾
We make reasonable efforts to keep the BC60 form current with the version published by the Colorado Real Estate Commission. However, real estate forms are subject to regulatory updates. You should always verify that the form version in the app matches the currently required version from your brokerage or the CREC before using it with clients. If you notice a discrepancy, please email us at hello@auaha.app.
Where is my agreement data stored? ▾
Agreement data is stored on your device. If you have cloud sync enabled, it is also backed up to your secure Firebase account (Google Cloud). Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not sell or share your data. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
What happens if I lose my phone or get a new one? ▾
If cloud sync is enabled, simply log in on your new device and your agreements will restore automatically. If you were using local-only storage, agreements stored on the old device will not transfer. We recommend enabling cloud sync to protect your data.
Can I share or export a completed agreement? ▾
Yes. Completed agreements can be exported as PDFs and shared via the native iOS or Android share sheet — email, AirDrop, messaging apps, or saved to your Files app.
I forgot my password. How do I reset it? ▾
On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password" and enter your email address. We'll send you a password reset link.
How do I delete my account? ▾
To delete your account and all associated data, email us at hello@auaha.app with the subject "Account Deletion Request." We will process your request within 30 days.
Does Threshold work offline? ▾
Yes. You can view and fill out agreements without a network connection. An internet connection is required for login, account creation, and syncing data to the cloud.
The app is crashing or not working correctly — what should I do? ▾
First, try closing and reopening the app. If the problem persists, try uninstalling and reinstalling. If you're still experiencing issues, email us at hello@auaha.app with a description of what's happening and what device and OS version you're using. Screenshots or screen recordings are very helpful.
