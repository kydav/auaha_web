Privacy Policy

Applies to: Threshold  ·  Last updated: June 16, 2026  ·  Auaha App Development LLC

This Privacy Policy describes how Auaha App Development LLC ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Threshold mobile application ("the App"). We are committed to protecting the privacy of our users.

1. Information We Collect

Threshold collects the minimum information necessary to provide its services:

  • Account information: Your name and email address, used to create and authenticate your agent account via Firebase Authentication.
  • Agreement data: Buyer agreement form data you enter within the App (such as buyer names, property addresses, and agreement terms). This data is stored on your device and, if you choose to sync, in your Firebase account.
  • Usage analytics: Anonymous, aggregated usage data to help us improve the App (e.g., feature usage frequency). This does not include any personal or client information.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect client personal information beyond what you enter into agreement forms.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data or your clients' data with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • We do not use your data to serve advertisements.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Authenticate and maintain your agent account
  • Store and retrieve your agreement data
  • Improve App performance and reliability
  • Respond to support requests you initiate

4. Data Storage and Security

Agreement data is stored primarily on your device. If cloud sync is enabled, data is stored in Firebase (Google Cloud), which is encrypted in transit and at rest. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Your account is protected by Firebase Authentication. We recommend using a strong, unique password and enabling any available multi-factor authentication.

5. Third-Party Services

Threshold uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): Used for authentication and optional cloud storage. See Firebase Privacy Policy.

6. Your Client's Data

You, as an agent using Threshold, are responsible for ensuring you have appropriate authorization to enter your clients' personal information into the App and for complying with any applicable real estate privacy and confidentiality obligations. Threshold provides the tools; you remain responsible for how you use them.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Agreement data stored on your device remains there until you delete it or uninstall the App. If you delete your account, your cloud-synced data will be deleted from our servers within 30 days. To request account deletion, contact us at hello@auaha.app.

8. Children's Privacy

Threshold is intended for use by licensed real estate professionals and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, contact us at hello@auaha.app.
