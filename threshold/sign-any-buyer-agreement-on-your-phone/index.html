Sign Any Buyer Agreement on Your Phone

Your state, board or brokerage decides which buyer agreement you use. Threshold works with that form: scan or upload it once, then fill it in and get it signed at every showing.

Get it on the App Store Get it on Google Play

Why your own form

Since August 2024, agents working through an MLS need a written buyer agreement signed before the first showing. The rule is national, but the form isn't: state associations, local boards and brokerages each publish their own, and many require a specific one. An app that only offers its own template doesn't help when your broker says "use ours." Threshold reads the form you're required to use and turns it into a guided, sign-on-the-phone agreement.

How it works

1. Add your form once

Scan the paper copy with your camera or upload the PDF, up to 30 pages including addenda. You review every scanned page first and can retake a blurry one. Threshold finds every blank, checkbox, initial and signature line. On fillable PDFs, fields line up exactly with the form's own blanks.

2. Check it and add your details

Confirm what each field is, then enter your name, brokerage and the terms you usually use, like your fee and protection period. Every deal on this form starts with them.

3. Fill it in at the showing

Add the buyers, the end date and where you represent them. Names, dates beside signatures and your details fill themselves in. Most agreements take under a minute.

4. Hand the buyer your phone

Buyers read a short plain-English overview of what they're agreeing to, right above the real agreement, then initial and sign on the screen. No app or account for them.

5. Signed and sent

You sign last and everyone gets the signed PDF by email. No signal at the property? Filling and signing still work, and the copies send once you're back online.

Questions

Can I use my own buyer agreement form in Threshold?

Yes. Scan the paper copy or upload the PDF your state association, board or brokerage requires. Threshold finds the blanks, checkboxes, initials and signature lines, you check them once, and the form is ready for every deal after.

Does Threshold work in my state?

Yes. Because you bring the form, Threshold works in every state. Colorado, Oklahoma, Louisiana and Wisconsin also have a built-in state form, ready the moment you sign up.

How long can the form be?

Up to 30 pages, including multi-page packets with addenda. Each page is shown for review before it's read, so you can retake a blurry page.

Do buyers need to download an app to sign?

No. You hand the buyer your phone. They read a short plain-English overview of the agreement, then initial and sign on the screen, and everyone gets the signed PDF by email.

Does it work without cell signal?

Filling and signing work with no signal. The signed copies send once you're back online. Adding a new form needs a connection, so do that before you head out.

What happens to the form I upload?

The pages are read on our servers with the help of Anthropic's Claude AI to find the fields, then deleted. Only the field layout is kept. Nothing you or your buyers write on a form is shared.
