The three ways buyer-agent compensation works now

Since the 2024 changes, how a buyer's agent gets paid is negotiated up front and written into the buyer agreement. There are three common structures:

  • Seller / listing broker pays. The listing side offers cooperating compensation; it usually doesn't come out of the buyer's pocket.
  • Buyer pays directly. The buyer agrees to pay their agent, and can ask the seller to contribute as part of the offer.
  • Seller concession. A negotiated seller credit helps cover the buyer's costs and the agent's fee.

Whatever you agree, it has to be specific

The written buyer agreement must state a specific, objective compensation amount — not open-ended — signed before you tour a home. That's the number this tool helps you state and explain.
