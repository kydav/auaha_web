What changed for Louisiana agents

Louisiana agents already work within the Louisiana Real Estate Commission's agency rules. Since August 17, 2024, agents working through an MLS also need a signed buyer representation agreement before showing a home. Threshold lets you handle disclosure and the written agreement together, right when you meet the buyer.

What your written buyer agreement needs

  • A signature before touring. Agents working through an MLS need the agreement signed before showing a home, in person or virtually.
  • A specific compensation amount. The figure must be objective and agreed with your buyer — not open-ended, and not more than you and the buyer set.
  • Clear terms and dates. The scope of representation and the time period should be spelled out, using written buyer representation agreement.
  • The buyer's signature on file. Keep a signed copy you can produce for your brokerage or a compliance check.
