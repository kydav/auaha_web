What's the best farrier app for a one-person business?

One that keeps your book organized and is fast to use between horses, without back-office overhead you don't need. Shoein' is built for exactly that — clients, horses, cycle reminders, and a map — free to start.

Do solo farriers need farrier software?

You don't need enterprise software, but a simple app beats texts and notebooks for staying on cycle and routing your day. The key is picking something you'll actually keep up to date.
