What 'free' should get you

A free farrier app should still cover the essentials: every client and horse in one place, reminders so no one slips past their cycle, and a way to reach clients and navigate your day. The paid platforms add invoicing, accounting sync, and route AI — useful if you need a full back office, but not required just to stay organized.
