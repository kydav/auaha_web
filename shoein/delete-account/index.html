Account

Delete your Shoein' account

Shoein' is made by Auaha App Development LLC. You can delete your account and its data at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Shoein' and sign in.
  2. Go to Profile and tap Delete account (below Sign out).
  3. Type DELETE to confirm.

Your account and data are deleted right away. If you signed in a long time ago, you may be asked to sign in again first.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email hello@auaha.app from the email address on your account, with the subject "Delete my Shoein' account". We'll confirm it's you, delete the account within 30 days, and let you know when it's done.

What's deleted

  • Your account and sign-in
  • All clients, horses, visits, appointments and mileage
  • All horse and visit photos
  • Your business settings (business name, prices, payment details)

What's kept

  • The date your free trial started, stored under an anonymous account ID with nothing else attached. Nothing about you or your clients is kept.
  • Purchase records held by Apple or Google under their own policies.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account doesn't cancel a Shoein' subscription. Cancel it in your App Store settings (iPhone) or Google Play subscriptions (Android) so you aren't charged again.

Questions? Email hello@auaha.app. See our Privacy Policy for more.
