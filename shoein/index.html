Why Shoein'

Your Whole Book, Organized

Stop digging through texts and notebooks. Shoein' puts every client, horse, and address where you can reach it in a tap.

📇

Client Book

Every client with their phone, email, address, and notes — all searchable, so you find anyone in seconds.

🐴

Every Horse

Track each horse under its owner — breed, notes, and the last service date, right where you need it.

⏱️

Never Miss a Cycle

A due badge flags horses that are coming up or overdue, so you know exactly who to call next.

🗺️

Map of Your Route

Every client with an address drops a pin on the map. See your day at a glance and tap to open.

📞

One-Tap Contact

Call, text, or get driving directions to a client from their card — no copying numbers or addresses.

🔒

Private & Secure

Your book is sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection and stored securely with Google Firebase. No ads, ever.

How It Works

Up and Running in Minutes

No setup headaches. Add your clients once and Shoein' keeps everything in order from there.

1

Create a Free Account

Sign up in seconds with your email — your book syncs securely to your account.

2

Add Your Clients

Enter each client's name, address, phone, and notes. The address drops a pin on your map automatically.

3

Add Their Horses

List each horse with its breed, notes, and last service date so you can track every cycle.

4

Work Your Day

Check who's due, open the map, and call, text, or navigate to your next stop in a single tap.
