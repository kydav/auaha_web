Pick for how you actually work

If you want an all-in-one back office — invoicing, accounting sync, route AI — the heavier platforms deliver that, at a monthly cost and a bit of a learning curve. If what you really need is your book organized (every client and horse, who's due next, and how to reach them), a simple, fast app wins, because you'll actually keep it up to date.

That's the gap Shoein' is built for: the core farrier book, done exceptionally well, free to start. It's a newer app launching soon, focused on being the fastest way to keep your whole book in your pocket rather than the most feature-stuffed.

Frequently asked

What is the best farrier app?

It depends on how you work. For an all-in-one back office with invoicing, accounting, and route AI, the heavier paid platforms fit. For a simple, fast book of clients, horses, and cycle reminders you'll actually keep current, Shoein' is built for exactly that — and it's free to start.

What should I look for in a farrier app?

Speed of use from the truck, clients and horses in one place, reminders so you never miss a cycle, a map of your addresses, one-tap contact, and a price that fits your book. Match the tool to how you actually work rather than to the longest feature list.

Is there a simple farrier app that isn't overkill?
