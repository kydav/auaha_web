Why it matters

The farriers who stay booked aren't just good under a horse — they're organized. When your whole book is in one place, you stop losing clients to missed cycles, you route your day efficiently, and you look professional every time you follow up.

Shoein' is built around exactly this: clients and horses together, last-service tracking with a due badge, addresses on a map, and one-tap contact — the farrier's book, in your pocket.

Frequently asked

How do farriers keep track of clients and horses?

The reliable approach is one system for the whole book — clients, their horses, addresses, and last-service dates — instead of texts and notebooks. Shoein' keeps all of it in one searchable place with cycle reminders.

What's the best app for managing a farrier client list?

Look for searchable client and horse records, cycle reminders, a map of addresses, and one-tap contact. Shoein' is built specifically for a farrier's client and horse book.

How do I stop losing clients to missed cycles?

Track each horse's last service date and let the app flag the next one. Shoein' shows a due badge on every horse that's coming up or overdue, so no one slips through.
