How to choose

If you want a full back office — automated invoicing, accounting sync, and AI route optimization — and you're happy to pay monthly and spend time setting it up, a heavier platform like FarrierIQ is aimed at that.

If what you actually need is your book organized and fast to use — every client and horse, who's due next, and how to reach them — Shoein' is built for that, and it's free to start. Pick based on whether you want an all-in-one system or a simple, focused book you'll keep current.

Frequently asked

Is Shoein' or FarrierIQ better?

Neither is universally better. FarrierIQ is a paid, feature-heavy platform for farriers who want an all-in-one back office. Shoein' is a simple, free-to-start book focused on clients, horses, cycle reminders, and your route. Choose based on how much you want to manage.

How much does FarrierIQ cost?

FarrierIQ lists a paid plan around $39/month on its site (verify current pricing there). Shoein' is free to start.

What's a simpler alternative to FarrierIQ?

If FarrierIQ feels like more than you need, Shoein' focuses on the core farrier book — clients, horses, cycle tracking, map, and one-tap contact — kept simple and free to start.

Keep your whole book in your pocket

Shoein' keeps every client and horse in one place — addresses on a map, one-tap call and directions, and a due badge so you never miss a cycle.
