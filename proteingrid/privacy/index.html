Privacy Policy

Applies to: ProteinGrid  ·  Last updated: June 20, 2026  ·  Auaha App Development LLC

This Privacy Policy explains how ProteinGrid ("the App"), developed by Auaha App Development LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), handles your information. We are committed to building software that respects your privacy.

1. What the App Does

ProteinGrid helps you log your daily protein intake, set a daily goal, track streaks, and view your history. All data is stored locally on your device.

2. Data We Collect

ProteinGrid does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information on external servers. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-in is required.
  • Your protein logs, daily goal, and history are stored only in local device storage (Hive database) and are never sent anywhere.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, location, health data, or any other personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or third-party tracking.

3. Local Notifications

If you enable reminders, the App schedules local notifications on your device. These notifications are generated and displayed entirely on your device — no notification content is sent to our servers or any third party.

4. Device Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

  • Notifications — to send you optional daily reminders to log protein.
  • Schedule Exact Alarm (Android) — to deliver reminders at the time you choose.

These permissions are optional. The App's core functionality works without them.

5. Data Export

The App includes a share feature that lets you export a summary of your logs using your device's native share sheet. You control where this data is sent; we have no involvement in or visibility into that sharing.

6. Children's Privacy

ProteinGrid is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because no data is collected at all, there is no data for us to have about any user, including children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this URL with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at hello@auaha.app.
