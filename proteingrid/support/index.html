Frequently Asked Questions

How do I change my daily protein goal?

Tap the settings icon in the top-right corner of the home screen. You can update your goal there at any time.

Why did my data disappear?

ProteinGrid stores all data locally on your device. If you deleted and reinstalled the App, uninstalling removes your local data. If you switched devices, your logs do not automatically transfer — the App is intentionally offline and does not sync to the cloud.

How do I use the home screen shortcuts?

On iOS, long-press the ProteinGrid app icon and select a quick-log action (e.g., "Log 30g"). On Android, long-press the icon to see the same shortcuts. These let you add a common amount without opening the app.

How do I turn off notifications?

Go to Settings within the App and toggle reminders off. You can also revoke notification permission at any time through your device's system settings under Apps > ProteinGrid > Notifications.

Can I export my data?

Yes. In the History screen, tap the share icon to export a summary of your logs using your device's share sheet.

How do I delete all my data?

Uninstalling the App from your device permanently removes all locally stored data.
