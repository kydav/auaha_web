Why Prior

Built for Utah Land

Water is the most important resource in Utah real estate. Prior puts water rights data right in your hands.

💧

Water Rights Data

Look up Points of Diversion (PODs) tied to any parcel — wells, diversions, and canal rights — sourced directly from the Utah Division of Water Resources.

🗺️

Interactive Parcel Map

Tap any parcel on the map to instantly see its boundaries, ownership type, and water rights. Switch between satellite, terrain, and standard views.

📍

Search Any Way You Want

Search by street address, GPS coordinates, or parcel number (APN). The map flies to your result and highlights the exact parcel.

📐

PLSS Legal Descriptions

See the Public Land Survey System (section, township, range) description for any location — the same format used in Utah deeds and title work.

🏡

Full Parcel Details

View acreage, assessed market value, building square footage, year built, subdivision name, and more from Utah's county LIR data.

📡

Live Government Data

Prior queries the Utah SGID statewide parcel layer and the Utah DNR ArcGIS services in real time — no stale cached databases.
