Privacy Policy

Applies to: Prior  ·  Last updated: June 25, 2026  ·  Auaha App Development LLC

This Privacy Policy describes how Auaha App Development LLC ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Prior mobile application ("the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy.

1. Information We Collect

Prior collects the minimum information necessary to provide its services:

  • Account information: Your email address, used to create and authenticate your account via Firebase Authentication.
  • Location data: If you use the "current location" feature, the App accesses your device's GPS coordinates to center the map. Location data is used only in the moment and is never stored or transmitted to our servers.
  • Search queries: Addresses, coordinates, or parcel numbers you enter are sent to third-party mapping and government data services to perform the lookup. These queries are not linked to your identity on our end.
  • Usage analytics: Anonymous, aggregated usage data to help us improve the App (e.g., feature usage frequency). This does not include any personal information.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not store your search history or the properties you look up.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.
  • We do not use your data to serve advertisements.
  • We do not collect or store your precise location persistently.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Authenticate and maintain your account
  • Provide water rights and parcel lookup results
  • Improve App performance and reliability
  • Respond to support requests you initiate

4. Third-Party Data Services

Prior queries government and mapping APIs to retrieve parcel and water rights data. When you perform a search, your query (address, coordinates, or parcel number) is sent to these services:

  • Mapbox: Used for map display, geocoding (address to coordinates), and map tile serving. See Mapbox Privacy Policy.
  • Utah SGID / AGRC ArcGIS: Utah state government parcel data services. Queries are subject to Utah's open government data policies.
  • Utah Division of Water Resources ArcGIS: Utah state government water rights (Points of Diversion) data. Queries are subject to Utah's open government data policies.

5. Account Security and Storage

Your account is managed by Firebase Authentication (Google LLC). We implement reasonable security measures to protect your account information. We recommend using a strong, unique password. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Account information is retained until you delete your account. To request account deletion, contact us at hello@auaha.app. Your account and associated data will be deleted within 30 days.

7. Third-Party Services

Prior uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policies:

  • Firebase (Google LLC): Authentication and analytics. See Firebase Privacy Policy.
  • Mapbox: Map display and geocoding. See Mapbox Privacy Policy.

8. Children's Privacy

Prior is intended for use by real estate professionals and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, contact us at hello@auaha.app.
