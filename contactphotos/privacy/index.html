Privacy Policy

Applies to: Contact Photos  ·  Last updated: June 11, 2026  ·  Auaha App Development LLC

This Privacy Policy explains how Contact Photos ("the App"), developed by Auaha App Development LLC ("we," "us," or "our"), collects, uses, and handles your information. We are committed to building software that respects your privacy.

1. What the App Does

Contact Photos helps you identify companies that text you, find their logo, and create a contact on your device with that logo. All contact creation happens locally on your device.

2. Information We Access and Collect

2a. Contacts

The App requests permission to read and write your device contacts. This access is used solely to create new contacts you explicitly request. We do not transmit, store, or share any of your contact data. Contact information never leaves your device.

2b. Company Search Queries

When you search for a company, the text you enter may be sent to Google's Gemini AI via Firebase AI to identify likely matching companies. Google processes this query according to their own privacy policy. We do not log or store search queries on our servers.

The App also makes direct HTTP requests to candidate company websites (e.g., example.com) to verify they exist and fetch logo images. These requests appear as standard web traffic from a mobile browser.

2c. Logo Images

To find a company's logo, the App may make requests to:

  • The company's own website
  • Third-party logo services: LogoKit (img.logokit.com) and Apistemic (logos-api.apistemic.com)
  • logos-world.net
  • Public social media profile pages (Facebook, Instagram) for companies that use social logos

These requests are made to download image files only. We do not share any personal data with these services.

2d. Analytics Data

The App uses Firebase Analytics (provided by Google) to collect anonymous usage data. This helps us understand how people use the App so we can improve it. Analytics data may include:

  • App events (e.g., which screens are visited, how often searches are performed)
  • Device information (e.g., device model, operating system version)
  • App version and session duration
  • General geographic region (country or region level — not precise location)
  • Crash reports and performance data

Analytics data is collected anonymously and is not linked to your identity or your contact data. You may opt out of analytics data collection through your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising; Android: Settings → Google → Manage your Google account → Data & Privacy). Google processes analytics data according to their Privacy Policy.

3. Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not create user accounts or require sign-in
  • We do not read your SMS or iMessage content
  • We do not store your contacts or personal data on our servers
  • We do not sell your data to third parties
  • We do not collect precise location data

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services, each governed by their own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase / Gemini AI — processes company search queries and powers analytics
  • LogoKit — logo image provider
  • Apistemic — logo image provider

5. Data Retention

We do not store personal data on our servers. Analytics data retained by Firebase follows Google's standard retention policies, which you can review in their documentation. Contact data you create via the App is stored locally on your device under your device's own data policies.

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including the right to access, correct, or request deletion of data held about you. Because we do not store personal data on our servers, most requests are satisfied by managing your device or your Google account settings. For any questions or requests, contact us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has used the App and provided personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top will reflect any changes. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy. For significant changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify you through the App.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at hello@auaha.app.
