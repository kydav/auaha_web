Frequently Asked Questions

How does Contact Photos find company logos?

When you search for a company, the app uses AI (Google Gemini via Firebase) to identify the most likely matching companies. It then fetches logo images directly from the company's website and from third-party logo services (LogoKit and Apistemic).

All of this happens on-device — no data about your contacts is ever sent to our servers.

Why does the app need access to my contacts?

Contact Photos needs contacts permission to save the logo you choose as a new contact on your device. The app reads your existing contacts only to avoid creating duplicates. Your contact data never leaves your device.

What's included in the free version?

You can add logos for up to 3 contacts for free. After that, a one-time $1.99 purchase unlocks unlimited contacts — no subscription, no recurring charges.

I paid but the unlock isn't showing. What do I do?

First, try tapping Restore Purchases in the app's settings. Your purchase is tied to your App Store or Google Play account, so restoring it on a new device or after reinstalling should work automatically.

If the restore option doesn't fix it, email us at hello@auaha.app with your order confirmation and we'll sort it out.

The logo it found doesn't look right. Can I change it?

Yes — after the search results appear, you can swipe through the logo options or search again with a different company name to get a better match. If no good result is found, you can skip saving a logo and create the contact without one.

Does the app read my text messages?

No. Contact Photos does not have access to your messages and cannot read your SMS or iMessage content. You manually type or paste the company name you want to look up — the app never reads your inbox.

Will the logo show up in my Messages app?

Yes — because the logo is saved as a standard contact photo on your device, it appears anywhere your device shows contact info, including the Messages app, Phone app, and any other app that reads your contacts.

How do I delete a contact created by the app?

Contacts are saved directly to your device's contacts — just open the Contacts app on your phone and delete them like any other contact. The app doesn't maintain a separate database.

I found a bug or have a feature request.

We'd love to hear it — email us at hello@auaha.app with as much detail as you can (device, OS version, what happened vs. what you expected). Feature requests are also very welcome.
