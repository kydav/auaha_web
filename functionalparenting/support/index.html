Contact Us

Can't find your answer below? Email us at hello@auaha.app and we'll get back to you as soon as we can.

In a crisis? Functional Parenting is a coaching tool, not an emergency service. If you or your child are in danger or crisis, contact a qualified professional or your local emergency number right away. In the U.S., you can call or text 988 to reach the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who is Functional Parenting for? ▾
It's for parents and caregivers who want practical, everyday support in understanding their child's behavior and responding with more calm and confidence. It's designed to fit into a busy day — small steps, not a big overhaul.
Is this therapy or medical advice? ▾
No. Functional Parenting is an educational and coaching tool. It is not therapy, counseling, or medical care, and it is not a substitute for advice from a licensed professional who knows your family. If you have concerns about your child's development or wellbeing, please consult a qualified professional. See our Terms for details.
How do I create an account? ▾
Open the app and tap "Create a free account." Enter your name, email, and a password, and you're in. The free version includes daily tips, challenges, reflections, and core tools.
Is my information private and secure? ▾
Yes. Everything is sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection and stored securely with Google Firebase, encrypted in transit and at rest. We don't show ads and we never sell your data. See our Privacy Policy for the full details.
Do I have to enter information about my child? ▾
No. The app doesn't require your child's name or any identifying details. Reflections and notes you write are optional and are stored privately under your account.
How do the daily tips and reminders work? ▾
Each day the app surfaces a fresh tip, a small challenge, and a reflection prompt. You can turn notifications on or off from within the app or in your device's notification settings.
What's free and what's paid? ▾
The core daily experience and tools are free. Optional purchases unlock the full toolkit and the self-paced course. Prices are always shown before you buy, and no payment information is needed to use the free version.
How do I restore a purchase on a new device? ▾
Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you used to buy, and use the "Restore purchases" option on the plans screen. Your access will be restored.
How do I cancel a subscription? ▾
iOS: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Functional Parenting → cancel. Android: Google Play → profile icon → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → cancel Functional Parenting.
How do I delete my account? ▾
Email us at hello@auaha.app with the subject "Account Deletion Request." We'll delete your account and associated data within 30 days.
The app is crashing or acting up — what should I do? ▾
First, close and reopen the app. If that doesn't help, try uninstalling and reinstalling. Still stuck? Email hello@auaha.app with a description of the problem and your device and OS version. Screenshots or a screen recording help a lot.
