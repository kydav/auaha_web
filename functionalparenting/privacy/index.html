Privacy Policy

Applies to: Functional Parenting  ·  Last updated: July 29, 2026  ·  Auaha App Development LLC

This Privacy Policy explains how Auaha App Development LLC ("we," "us," or "our") handles information in connection with the Functional Parenting mobile application ("the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and collecting as little information as possible.

1. Information We Collect

Functional Parenting collects the minimum information needed to provide its services:

  • Account information: Your name and email address, used to create and authenticate your account through Firebase Authentication.
  • Content you create: Reflections, notes, challenge progress, and similar entries you choose to save in the App. These are associated with your account.
  • Purchase status: If you buy the toolkit or course, we receive a record that the purchase was made. Payments are processed by Apple or Google, and purchase records are managed on our behalf by RevenueCat (see Third-Party Services below). We never see or store your payment card details.
  • Usage & analytics data: Through Firebase Analytics we collect information about how the App is used — such as which screens and features you open, session activity, and general device information (like device model and operating-system version). This is tied to a randomly generated, app-specific identifier rather than to your name, and we use it in aggregate to understand and improve the App.
  • Diagnostic & crash data: If the App crashes or encounters an error, Firebase Crashlytics collects diagnostic information — such as the type of crash, device model, operating-system version, app state at the time, and a randomly generated crash-instance identifier — so we can find and fix problems.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require you to enter your child's name or identifying details to use the App.
  • We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own advertising.
  • We do not use your information to serve advertisements in the App.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or location.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely to:

  • Create and authenticate your account
  • Save and sync the content and progress you create
  • Deliver the daily tips, challenges, and (if you enable them) notifications
  • Provide access to features you have purchased
  • Improve the App's performance and content
  • Respond to support requests you send us

4. Secure Transmission & Storage

All communication between the App and our servers is encrypted in transit using industry-standard HTTPS/TLS. Your account and content are stored with Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore), where data is encrypted both in transit and at rest. We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is ever 100% secure, but we work to protect your data using current best practices.

Your account is protected by Firebase Authentication. We recommend choosing a strong, unique password.

5. Third-Party Services

Functional Parenting relies on the following third-party services, each governed by its own privacy policy:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): authentication, secure cloud storage (Cloud Firestore), notifications, usage analytics (Firebase Analytics), and crash/diagnostic reporting (Firebase Crashlytics). See the Firebase Privacy and Security documentation and the Google Privacy Policy. Firebase's handling of data is described in its terms, including how it processes and secures data on Google Cloud infrastructure.
  • RevenueCat, Inc.: manages in-app purchase records and entitlements. When you make or restore a purchase, RevenueCat receives the purchase receipt, an app-specific user identifier, and basic device information so it can confirm what you own. It does not receive your payment card details. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play: handle any purchases. We do not receive your payment details.
  • Taylor Thomas Coaching LLC (opt-in only): A separate company that provides the optional coaching, workshops, and email list. If you request the free guide, join the email list, book a call, or register for a workshop, your name and email are shared with it and its email-marketing and scheduling tools (such as LeadConnector / HighLevel) and third-party video tools. See "Optional Email Opt-In & Coaching Requests" below.

6. Optional Email Opt-In & Coaching Requests

Some features let you connect with the coaching side of Functional Parenting — for example, requesting the free guide, joining the email list, booking a free call, or registering for a live workshop. These services are provided by Taylor Thomas Coaching LLC, a separate company from Auaha App Development LLC, and are entirely optional.

If you use one of them, we pass your name and email address to Taylor Thomas Coaching LLC and its email-marketing and scheduling tools (such as LeadConnector / HighLevel) so it can send you what you requested and, if you opted in, occasional related emails. Booking a call or joining a workshop may also involve third-party scheduling or video tools, and any details you enter there are governed by those services' policies. Information you share with Taylor Thomas Coaching LLC is handled under its own policies. You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time using the link in any email, or by contacting us. If you never use these features, none of this information is shared.

7. Not a Healthcare Service

Functional Parenting is an educational and coaching tool. It is not a medical, mental-health, or therapy service, it does not create a provider–patient or therapist–client relationship, and the information you enter is not treated as medical records.

Because the App does not provide licensed healthcare services and is not offered by a covered entity acting in a clinical capacity, health-privacy laws such as HIPAA do not apply to it. That said, we protect your information using strong security practices, including the encrypted transmission and storage described above. If you use the App alongside professional care, any information shared with your own licensed provider is governed by that provider's separate policies, not this one.

8. Information About Your Children

The App is intended for use by parents and caregivers (adults). It is not directed to children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The App does not require you to enter identifying information about your child. If you choose to write reflections or notes that mention your child, that content is stored under your account and treated with the same protections as your other content. Please avoid entering more sensitive detail than you need.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain your account and content for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and its associated data directly in the App at any time (in your account settings), or you can request deletion by emailing hello@auaha.app. We will process email deletion requests within 30 days.

10. Your Choices

  • You can turn notifications on or off in the App or in your device settings.
  • You can edit or delete content you have created, and delete your account from within the App.
  • You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time via the link in any email.
  • You can request a copy or deletion of your data by contacting us.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will note significant changes by updating the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, contact us at hello@auaha.app.
