Frequently asked

Can one app work on both iPhone and Android?

Yes — a cross-platform app is built from a single codebase that runs natively on both iOS and Android, so you reach every user without building two separate apps.

Do you handle App Store and Google Play submission?

Yes — we handle the build, store setup, and release process for both the Apple App Store and Google Play.

Will the app feel native on both?

Yes — we build with Flutter, which compiles to native code, so the app is fast and feels right on both platforms.
