Frequently asked

I have an app idea — what's the first step?

Write down what problem it solves and for whom, then reach out. We start with a discovery conversation to turn the idea into a clear plan before any building begins.

How do I know what my app should include?

We help you find the core — the one or two things it must do — and start there. That keeps the first version focused, affordable, and fast to launch.

How long does it take to build an app?

It depends on scope, which is why we scope it with you up front. Starting with a focused first version is the fastest path to a launched app.

Have a project in mind?

Book a free 30-minute call. Bring a full spec or a napkin idea — you'll leave knowing what it would take and roughly what it would cost, including if we think you don't need an app at all.
